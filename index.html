MannMukti means “mental liberation.”

We're the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign chapter of MannMukti, a community dedicated to encouraging open conversations about mental health in South Asian communities, reducing stigma, and creating space for self-care.

Through cultural celebrations, events, and conversations, we want everyone to feel like there's a place for them here.
