MannMukti means “mental liberation.”

Through cultural celebrations, events, and conversations, we want everyone to feel like there's a place for them here.

MannMukti is an organization that aims to encourage healthy, open dialogue of mental health issues in the South Asian community in an effort to remove stigma, improve awareness and promote self-care. All events and social posts by MannMukti will be delivered to this mission. Moreover, MannMukti UIUC is the largest college chapter of the MannMukti foundation
